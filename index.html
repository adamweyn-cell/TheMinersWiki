The Ender Dragon

Boss Mob • Version 1.20

The Ender Dragon is a giant flying boss mob found when first entering the End dimension. It is the largest naturally spawning mob in the game and guards the exit portal back to the Overworld.

The Caves & Cliffs

Major Update • Version 1.18

The Caves & Cliffs update completely overhauled Overworld terrain generation, introducing massive lush caves, deep dripstone caverns, towering jagged peaks, and new ore distribution levels.

The Caves & Cliffs Update

Community Event • Version 1.20

Did You Know?
  • Creepers were originally created by accident when Notch tried to code a pig model and mixed up the height and length values.
  • Mining an Obsidian block requires a Diamond or Netherite pickaxe and takes 9.4 seconds without enchantments.
  • Endermen speak distorted English phrases played backward, such as "Here we go" and "What's up?".
  • A single bucket of lava can smelt up to 100 items in a furnace, making it the longest-lasting fuel source.
  • Cats can scare away Creepers and Phantoms, and they also prevent fall damage completely.
